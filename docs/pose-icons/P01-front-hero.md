PURPOSE

This is a technical pose-selection icon for a commercial saree e-commerce and AI fashion photography platform.

It is NOT a decorative fashion illustration.

The image must communicate one precise, repeatable pose:

“Neutral, elegant, straight-on, full-body saree catalogue hero pose.”

Prioritize pose clarity, garment visibility, anatomical readability, and minimalist UI design.

Body orientation:
- Exact frontal view
- Approximately 0° torso rotation
- Head facing directly forward
- Hips facing directly forward
- Torso upright and elongated
- Neck upright
- Shoulders relaxed and approximately level

Do not create:
- torso twisting
- body leaning

- runway stance
- walking motion
- editorial pose
- dramatic fashion pose

Do not use:
- hand on waist
- crossed arms
- raised arms

- dramatic gestures

Do not create detailed fingers or expressive hand gestures.

Do not create:
- overlapping feet
- one hidden foot

1. Saree body
2. Pallu
3. Blouse
4. Front pleats
5. Lower border

The saree is included only to explain garment placement relative to the pose.

It must remain minimal.


SAREE BODY

- Clean traditional saree silhouette
- Long vertical drape
- Naturally wrapped around body
- Saree body clearly visible

- Elegant straight silhouette

Do not create excessive folds, volume, or flowing fabric.

- Pallu crosses diagonally across torso
- Pallu rests naturally over one shoulder

Do not make the pallu:

- dramatically flowing
- excessively wide

- obscure hands

Do not create excessive folds.
