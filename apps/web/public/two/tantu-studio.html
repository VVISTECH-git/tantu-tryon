Tantu Studio FAQ

What is Tantu Studio?

Tantu Studio is Tantu's flagship multi-look campaign mode. You upload a collection of the same garment type, set creative direction, and AI plans plus generates a unified editorial photoshoot — full collection covers, combo covers, and individual look shots with per-look models, poses and aspect ratios.

How is Tantu Studio different from regular Tantu photoshoots?

Regular photoshoots generate up to 12 poses from one garment upload — ideal for single-SKU catalogues. Studio handles multi-look collections: AI creative planning, collection covers that stitch multiple looks together, combo frames, and per-look configuration. Studio is built for brands shipping coordinated drops, not one-off listings.

How many credits does Tantu Studio cost?

Studio campaigns charge 30 credits for AI creative planning plus per-shot generation (9 credits at 1K, 25 at 2K, 35 at 4K per image). A four-look campaign with a collection cover and a combo cover at 1K is 84 credits. Studio unlocks at 51+ credits, so any credit pack unlocks it.

Can Tantu Studio shoot saree and lehenga collections?

Yes. Studio supports Kurti Set, Saree, Lehenga, Men Ethnic, Western, Shirt, Trouser, Jewelry and Product categories. Saree and lehenga campaigns include blouse style control and drape-accurate prompts tuned for Indian ethnic wear.

Are Tantu Studio images accepted on Myntra, Amazon and Shopify?

Yes. Outputs render at marketplace-ready aspect ratios (1:1, 3:4, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9) up to 4K. Sellers use Studio heroes on websites and listing galleries; images include full commercial usage rights.

Does Tantu Studio keep the same model across all looks?

You choose. Each look can have its own model, pose and aspect ratio while the campaign shares one creative direction — scene, colour grade and styling mood — so the set reads as one photoshoot, not random AI images.

What is a collection cover vs a combo cover?

A collection cover shows all looks in one editorial hero frame — ideal for website banners and collection launch posts. A combo cover pairs two looks in one shot — ideal for teasers and carousel slides. Both are generated automatically in Studio.

Can I retry failed shots in Tantu Studio?

Yes. Individual failed shots can be retried from the results page without regenerating the entire campaign. Credits for failed generations are refunded automatically during the main generation pass.

Is Tantu Studio available internationally?

Yes. Studio runs in the web app worldwide. Pricing displays in INR for India and USD for international visitors. Credits never expire regardless of region.

Who should use Tantu Studio vs regular generation?

Use regular generation for single garments and fast SKU catalogues from 9 credits per photo. Use Studio when you launch multi-look collections, need cover and combo heroes, or want AI to plan an entire editorial campaign before shooting.
